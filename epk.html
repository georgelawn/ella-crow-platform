Bio

Ella Crow is a New York-raised singer-songwriter now based in London, UK. Her music sits at the crossroads of R&B, Americana, Folk, and Rock - anchored by powerhouse vocals and thought provoking lyrics. Ella writes with the kind of unflinching honesty that pulls listeners in immediately, pairing tell-all storytelling with dynamic, genre-bending melodies. Since arriving in London, she has been steadily building a presence on the live circuit, bringing a fresh perspective to the city's independent music scene.
